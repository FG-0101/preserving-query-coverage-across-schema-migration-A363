DSA Transparency Database (https://transparency.dsa.ec.europa.eu/), CC BY 4.0
unless otherwise indicated.  The 71 daily record reductions and the 21 monthly
complete aggregate reductions are shipped pre-reduced.  The acquisition step
that downloads and reduces the publisher files is not included and the run uses
no network access.  The publisher checksum records for the source archives are
kept in data/derived/dump_checksum_*.csv, data/derived/complete_checksums.csv
and data/archive_snapshot_lock.json.

code/fonts/OFL.txt; used only to render the figures.
